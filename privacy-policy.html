Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026

At CoreMetric Suite (accessible via CoreMetricSuite.com), safeguarding the privacy and operational confidentiality of our users is an absolute foundational priority. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we process, our architectural commitments to client-side data security, and how third-party advertising partners interact with your browser session.

1. Client-Side Processing & Zero Input Logging

CoreMetric Suite operates entirely on a serverless, static utility model. All numerical parameters, values, asset figures, dates, and calculations entered into our financial, legal, and operational tools (including tax calculators, rent trackers, and media validators) are processed exclusively within your browser using client-side JavaScript.

We do not capture, log, transmit, or store any of your private financial or legal inputs on any server. When you close or refresh your web browser, all calculated session metrics are immediately erased from active memory.

2. Log Files & Automated Technical Metrics

Like virtually all web applications, CoreMetric Suite utilizes standard server logs provided via our CDN infrastructure (Vercel Inc.). These log files collect non-personally identifiable technical information automatically transmitted by your web browser. This includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser type and user-agent string
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP) information
  • Date and time stamps
  • Referring and exit page paths

These metrics are utilized strictly to analyze system stability, prevent malicious traffic spikes, optimize page speed metrics, and administer global platform performance.

3. Cookies and Advertising Partners

CoreMetric Suite uses cookies to store user preferences and maintain core site functionality. Additionally, third-party vendors, including Google AdSense and programmatic media networks, serve advertisements on our platform based on user visits to this and other websites across the internet.

  • Third-party advertisers use cookies and web beacons to measure ad performance and serve tailored advertising content.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to CoreMetricSuite.com and other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info or adjusting their Google Ad Settings.

4. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) & GDPR Rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users retain specific data privacy rights. Because CoreMetric Suite does not collect personally identifiable information, sell personal data, or store user accounts, we inherently maintain zero persistent personal profile records.

If you submit an inquiry via email, your email address is used solely to respond to your specific request and is never shared, sold, or rented to third-party marketers.
