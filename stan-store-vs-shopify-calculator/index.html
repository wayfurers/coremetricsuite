Stan Store vs. Shopify Transaction Cut & Fee Calculator (2026 Updated)

When building a creator business, choosing the right eCommerce platform is critical to protecting your profit margins. While both Stan Store and Shopify provide excellent tools to sell digital products, courses, and memberships, their pricing structures and transaction fee models are radically different.

As of 2026, many creators are surprised to discover the hidden costs of third-party payment gateways, platform penalty percentages, and volume break-even points. This calculator breaks down the exact math so you can see your true net take-home pay based on your actual monthly revenue and average order value.

Understanding Stan Store Pricing & 0% Transaction Fees

Stan Store has built a reputation for straightforward, transparent pricing. In 2026, the platform offers two primary tiers:

  • Creator Plan ($29/month): Includes the core storefront, digital downloads, bookings, and basic lead capture.
  • Creator Pro Plan ($99/month): Unlocks advanced marketing funnels, upsells, order bumps, affiliate management, and pixel tracking.

The biggest advantage of Stan Store is that it charges a 0% platform transaction fee. However, "0% transaction fees" does not mean entirely free processing. You are still required to pay the standard credit card processing rates charged by Stripe or PayPal (typically 2.9% + 30¢ for standard US cards). Stan Store simply doesn't take an extra cut of your sales on top of what the payment processor requires.

Shopify Processing Rates & The Third-Party Gateway Penalty

Shopify's pricing model is built around its native payment gateway, Shopify Payments. As of 2026, the standard monthly plans and their associated online credit card processing rates are:

  • Basic Shopify ($39/month): 2.9% + 30¢ online rate.
  • Shopify Grow ($105/month): 2.7% + 30¢ online rate.
  • Advanced Shopify ($399/month): 2.5% + 30¢ online rate.

If you use Shopify Payments exclusively, Shopify does not charge any additional sales commission. However, if you choose to connect a third-party gateway (like an external Stripe account, Authorize.net, or independent high-risk processor), Shopify imposes a hefty third-party transaction fee penalty. This penalty is 2.0% on the Basic plan, 1.0% on Grow, and 0.6% on Advanced. This makes Stan Store significantly cheaper if you are forced to use an external processor.

Break-Even Math: When to Upgrade Your Shopify Plan

As your store scales, upgrading your Shopify plan can mathematically pay for itself through lower processing rates. For example, moving from Basic (2.9%) to Grow (2.7%) saves you 0.2% on every transaction. Because the Grow plan costs $66 more per month ($105 vs $39), the break-even volume on processing alone is roughly $33,000 per month. If you sell more than $33,000 monthly, the Grow plan is mathematically cheaper than Basic.

Stan Store Creator vs. Shopify Basic: Which is Better for You?

If you are a solo creator selling digital downloads, coaching, and courses primarily via a link-in-bio on Instagram or TikTok, Stan Store is often the superior choice. Its $29 entry point is lower than Shopify's $39 Basic plan, and its mobile-first checkout converts exceptionally well for social traffic.

Conversely, if you are building a massive catalog of physical products, require advanced inventory tracking, complex shipping logistics, and multi-channel fulfillment, Shopify remains the industry standard. While slightly more expensive at baseline, its App ecosystem and physical product management capabilities are unmatched.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Stan Store charge a transaction fee?
No, Stan Store charges a 0% platform transaction fee on both its $29 Creator and $99 Creator Pro plans. However, you are still responsible for standard payment processing fees (typically 2.9% + 30¢ via Stripe or PayPal) which apply to all eCommerce platforms.
What are Shopify's transaction fees in 2026?
If you use Shopify Payments, online credit card rates are 2.9% + 30¢ on Basic ($39/mo), 2.7% + 30¢ on Grow ($105/mo), and 2.5% + 30¢ on Advanced ($399/mo). If you use a third-party gateway instead of Shopify Payments, Shopify adds a penalty fee of 2.0% (Basic), 1.0% (Grow), or 0.6% (Advanced).
Is Stan Store cheaper than Shopify?
For new creators, Stan Store's Creator plan ($29/month) is generally cheaper than Shopify Basic ($39/month). Both charge an effective 2.9% + 30¢ for standard card processing via native solutions. Stan Store becomes much more cost-effective if you rely on a third-party payment processor, as Shopify heavily penalizes external gateways.
What is the third-party gateway penalty on Shopify?
If you process payments through a gateway other than Shopify Payments (such as a standalone Stripe account or Authorize.net), Shopify adds an extra transaction fee on top of your gateway's processing rate. This penalty is 2% on the Basic plan, 1% on the Grow plan, and 0.6% on the Advanced plan.
When should I upgrade my Shopify plan to save on transaction fees?
The break-even point to upgrade from Shopify Basic to Shopify Grow based purely on payment processing math is roughly $33,000 per month in revenue. At that volume, the 0.2% drop in transaction fees (from 2.9% to 2.7%) covers the $66 increase in the monthly subscription cost.
