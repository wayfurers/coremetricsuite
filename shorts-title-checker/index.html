The Mechanics of YouTube Shorts Title Truncation & Character Limits

While YouTube Studio enforces a strict 100-character hard limit for video titles across all formats, the physical screen real estate on mobile devices imposes a much tighter constraint. When a viewer scrolls through the YouTube Shorts feed on iOS or Android, the video player reserves significant screen space for right-hand action buttons (Like, Dislike, Comments, Share, Remix) and bottom channel branding elements.

As a result, YouTube Shorts titles truncate with an ellipsis (...) after approximately 48 to 55 characters on standard smartphones. If your core hook, primary keyword, or intriguing plot twist sits past character 55, it becomes completely invisible to viewers scrolling through their feed.

Strategic Hashtag Placement in YouTube Shorts Titles

A frequent mistake among new creators is prefixing titles with #Shorts or stacking four different hashtags inside the title field. While hashtags were required during the early rollout of YouTube Shorts, YouTube's neural algorithm now automatically identifies vertical 9:16 videos under 60 seconds as Shorts.

Adding #Shorts at the beginning of your title consumes 8 valuable characters (including spaces) from your visible mobile safe zone. If you choose to include hashtags for topic categorization, place 1 focused tag at the very end of your title after your primary hook, or place all hashtags inside the video description.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the hard character limit for YouTube Shorts titles in 2026?
The hard character ceiling for a YouTube Shorts title is 100 characters in YouTube Studio. This count includes letters, spaces, numbers, punctuation, and emojis.
Where do YouTube Shorts titles truncate on mobile screens?
On the native mobile YouTube Shorts player overlay, titles truncate with an ellipsis (...) after approximately 50 to 55 characters depending on character pixel width, font rendering, and whether hashtags are present.
Should I include #Shorts in my YouTube Shorts video title?
No, putting #Shorts in your title wastes valuable visible real estate. YouTube automatically categorizes vertical videos under 60 seconds as Shorts. It is better to place target hashtags in the description field or at the very end of the title after your core hook.
How does title length affect YouTube Shorts Click-Through-Rate (CTR)?
Titles that front-load their primary topic and emotional hook within the first 45-50 visible characters achieve up to 35% higher CTR on mobile home feeds and search results because viewers read the full value proposition without truncation.
How many power words or emojis should I use in a Shorts title?
The optimal density is 1 to 2 power words combined with 1 relevant emoji. Overloading titles with excessive capital letters or multiple emojis reduces readability and can trigger algorithmic spam penalties.
