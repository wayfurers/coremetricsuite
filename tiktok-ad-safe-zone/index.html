TikTok Ad Safe Area Dimensions Breakdown

Because mobile screen dimensions vary across modern smartphones, TikTok's interface adjusts slightly dynamically. Adhering to standardized safety margins ensures your message is never blocked:

Why Ad Overlay Safe Zones Matter for Campaign ROI

TikTok users decide whether to keep watching a video within the first 1.5 seconds. If key promotional hooks, product benefits, or price callouts are covered by comment icons or CTA buttons, conversion rates plummet.

  • Subtitle Legibility: Hardcoded captions placed too low will clash directly with TikTok's native account handle and multi-line descriptions.
  • Logo Placement: Placing brand logos in the top-right or bottom-right corners will result in direct clipping under profile icons and share buttons.
  • Cross-Platform Adaptation: Video ads repurposed from Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts often have slightly different safe margins; testing prevents campaign rejection or poor delivery.
