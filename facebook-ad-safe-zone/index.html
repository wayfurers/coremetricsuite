Facebook 9:16 In-Feed Ad Visual Mask & Safe Zone Guide

When launching vertical video or image advertisements across Facebook Reels and Stories, ensuring that critical hooks, brand logos, primary text captions, and Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons remain completely unobstructed is essential for campaign performance. Meta places heavy native user interface (UI) layers directly over full-screen 9:16 placements—including the advertiser profile badge, timestamp, audio details, and the prominent bottom interactive action bar.

This browser-based facebook-ad-safe-zone utility provides media buyers, performance marketers, and creative designers with an instant sandbox to test 1080x1920 pixel vertical ad layouts against official Meta placement boundaries before pushing campaigns live in Ads Manager.

Meta Vertical Ad Safe Area Dimensions Breakdown

To prevent key visual components from getting hidden behind native buttons or cropped by mobile screen aspect ratios, structure your creative compositions around these exact pixel clearances:

UI Boundary Region Pixel Offset (1080p) Risk Level Interface Component Notes
Top Header Margin 0 px – 270 px High Risk Reserved for advertiser profile icon, page name, close button, and story progress bars.
Bottom CTA Margin 1250 px – 1920 px High Risk Contains the "Sponsored" label, primary text caption snippet, audio info, and dynamic CTA button.
Side Margins (Left/Right) 0 px – 65 px Medium Risk Buffer zones protecting against device edge bezel cropping and swipe gesture triggers.
Central Safe Content Box 950 × 980 px Optimal The prime focal zone where hooks, product demonstrations, and text overlays are 100% visible.

Why Safe Zones Directly Impact Ad ROAS & CTR

Users scroll through Facebook Reels and Stories at high velocity. If your primary hook or product benefit text is obscured by the bottom CTA container or top profile handle, consumer comprehension drops immediately, causing ad fatigue and lower click-through rates (CTR).

  • Uninterrupted Messaging: Hardcoded subtitles or overlay callouts must sit well above the 670px lower threshold to prevent clashing with native Meta captions.
  • Brand Recall: Placing logos within the top 270px safety buffer ensures brand identity is never blocked by profile headers.
  • Cross-Placement Efficiency: Designing within a unified 9:16 master template allows your creative asset to serve Facebook Reels, Instagram Reels, and Stories without clipping errors.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the exact Facebook 9:16 safe zone dimensions for 1080x1920 video ads?
For 1080x1920 vertical Facebook Reels and Stories ads, keep essential text, logos, and hooks clear of the top 270px (profile info and header) and bottom 670px (captions, audio title, and CTA button). Additionally, maintain a 65px left and right margin to protect against edge cropping.
How does client-side frame checking protect my advertising creatives?
All uploaded video frames, mockup images, canvas rendering, and safe zone calculations are processed 100% locally inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas. No media files or campaign assets are ever uploaded to any remote server.
How do I use a safe zone template PNG in Premiere Pro or CapCut?
Click the 'Export 1080x1920 PNG Mask' button to download a transparent reference overlay template. Place this image on the topmost track of your video timeline while positioning text and subtitles, then remove or disable the track before final ad export.
Why are my Facebook Reels ad captions getting covered by the UI?
Meta's native interface overlays player controls, primary text, advertiser usernames, and call-to-action buttons across the bottom 35% of the screen. Hardcoded subtitles placed too low will clash with these elements, lowering user engagement.
What file formats and aspect ratios are supported by this mockup tool?
The utility supports standard image files (PNG, JPG) and vertical video containers (MP4, MOV) up to Meta's standard limits. A 9:16 vertical resolution (1080x1920 pixels) yields the most accurate preview results.
