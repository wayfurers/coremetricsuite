Engineering Deterministic Financial Utilities

CoreMetric Suite was founded to eliminate black-box financial estimation. Web-based business calculators frequently rely on simplified approximations or harvest submitted user metrics on remote database servers. Our platform engineered a client-side architecture where all mathematical logic executes directly within your local browser session.

Every tool hosted across our suite incorporates verified statutory frameworks—including IRS MACRS tables, UK Renters' Rights Act notice standards, and ATO tax brackets. Inputs are never logged, stored, or transmitted to any remote API. When you close or refresh your browser tab, all session values are erased.
