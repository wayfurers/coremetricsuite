What are the exact Instagram Reels safe zone dimensions for 1080x1920 videos?

Why does text get cropped when Instagram Reels appear in the main profile grid?
Instagram automatically crops 9:16 vertical video assets into a 4:5 aspect ratio card (1080x1350px) when displayed inside the home feed, and a 1:1 square thumbnail (1080x1080px) on profile grids.
