Frequently Asked Questions

What are the exact Instagram Reels safe zone dimensions for 1080x1920 videos?
For a standard 1080x1920 pixel (9:16) Instagram Reel, keep text inside a safe zone with a top margin of 220px, a bottom margin of 420px, a left margin of 44px, and a right margin of 130px.
Why does text get cropped when Instagram Reels appear in the main profile grid?
Instagram automatically crops 9:16 vertical video assets into a 4:5 aspect ratio card (1080x1350px) for feed previews and a 1:1 square thumbnail (1080x1080px) on profile grids. Text placed too high or low gets cut off in these non-full-screen views.
