LinkedIn Carousel Header & Footer Safe Zone Clearance Checker

Publishing high-converting PDF carousels on LinkedIn requires precise structural visual planning. While LinkedIn PDF carousels achieve higher engagement rates than single-image posts, creators frequently make layout mistakes by placing headers, subheadlines, brand logos, or calls-to-action too close to the top and bottom slide borders.

When a user opens a PDF document carousel inside LinkedIn's mobile application, the native document player automatically overlays a persistent dark gradient header bar (containing your PDF filename and full-screen expansion icon) and a bottom pagination counter (displaying page counts and navigation arrows). If your slide text sits inside these restricted overlay zones, critical information gets covered.

This web utility provides B2B marketers, designers, and content creators with an instant, privacy-focused visual validator for 1080x1350 (4:5) portrait and 1080x1080 (1:1) square carousel slides. Validate your margins, prevent UI collisions, and export clean alignment guides.

Why Header and Footer Clearance Matters for Engagement

A high-performing LinkedIn carousel hook relies on immediate legibility within the first two seconds of feed scrolling. Poor alignment harms performance in three key ways:

  • Obscured Hook Headlines: Placing your primary slide title in the top 90px means LinkedIn's native `.pdf` file name string will render directly over your text on mobile screens.
  • Blocked Brand Logos and Handles: Placing your company logo or handle in the top-right corner results in it being covered by the mobile full-screen expansion button.
  • Truncated Call-to-Actions (CTAs): Swipe arrows or "Link in Comments" prompts placed at the bottom border get covered by LinkedIn's native slide counter overlay (e.g., "1/10").

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the standard dimensions for LinkedIn Carousel PDF slides?
The recommended size for LinkedIn PDF document carousels is 1080x1350 pixels (4:5 portrait ratio) for maximum mobile feed real estate, or 1080x1080 pixels (1:1 square ratio) for universal desktop and mobile display.
How much top and bottom clearance should I leave on LinkedIn carousels?
For 1080x1350 vertical slides, maintain at least 90px top margin clearance for the document header bar (title and full-screen trigger) and 120px bottom margin clearance for the native page number counter and swipe control bar.
Does this tool upload my proprietary slide decks or PDF files to a server?
No. All image uploads, canvas rendering, safe zone grid calculations, and PDF frame previews are executed 100% locally within your browser using HTML5 Canvas APIs. No data is stored or transmitted.
Why are my slide headers getting cut off on mobile devices?
LinkedIn overlays its document title bar directly over the top of PDF uploads when viewed on mobile screens. Placing titles or logos within the top 90px of a 1350px canvas causes native text collision.
How can I export a reference template for Canva, Photoshop, or Figma?
Click the 'Export 1080x1350 PNG Preview' button to download a grid template. Import this image into Canva or Figma as an alignment overlay guide while designing your document slides.
