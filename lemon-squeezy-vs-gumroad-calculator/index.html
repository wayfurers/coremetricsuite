Mathematical Breakdown: Platform & Processing Fee Models

Understanding the real cost of selling digital goods requires separating published headline rates from total transaction fee stacks. Below is how both platforms calculate total platform deductions on a completed sale:

1. Lemon Squeezy Fee Architecture

Lemon Squeezy operates primarily on a bundled MoR structure:

Lemon Squeezy Transaction Fee = ( Gross Sales Volume × 5% ) + ( Total Orders × $0.50 ) + Surcharges

Surcharges = ( International Cards × 1.5% ) + ( PayPal Checkouts × 1.5% ) + ( Subscription Billing × 0.5% )

Crucially, Lemon Squeezy refunds its base platform fee when a customer requests a valid refund, protecting creators from losing fees on unfulfilled or returned orders.

2. Gumroad Fee Architecture

Gumroad utilizes a tiered structure depending on whether customer sales originate directly from your audience links or via the internal Gumroad Discover marketplace:

Direct Sales Fee = ( Direct Sales Volume × 10% ) + ( Direct Orders × $0.50 ) + Payment Processing (~2.9% + $0.30)

Discover Sales Fee = Discover Sales Volume × 30% (Flat rate, processing included)

In addition, Gumroad retains its full platform commission on refunded orders, meaning a refunded $100 sale results in a direct out-of-pocket loss of $10.50+ for the seller.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the key difference in platform fees between Lemon Squeezy and Gumroad in 2026?
Lemon Squeezy charges a base platform fee of 5% + $0.50 per transaction, whereas Gumroad charges a 10% + $0.50 base platform fee on direct sales. On top of that, Gumroad requires separate payment processing fees (~2.9% + $0.30 via Stripe or PayPal), while Lemon Squeezy bundles payment processing into its MoR fee structure.
Do both Lemon Squeezy and Gumroad handle global sales tax and VAT as a Merchant of Record?
Yes. Both Lemon Squeezy and Gumroad operate as Merchants of Record (MoR). They calculate, collect, and remit global EU/UK VAT, US sales tax, and GST automatically on your behalf, removing legal tax liability from digital creators.
How does Gumroad's Discover Marketplace impact total fee calculations?
If a customer purchases your digital product through Gumroad Discover (Gumroad's internal recommendation engine), Gumroad charges a flat 30% commission instead of the standard 10% direct fee. This includes processing, but significantly lowers net payout margins.
At what monthly revenue threshold should a digital creator switch from Gumroad to Lemon Squeezy?
The financial tipping point usually occurs between $1,000 and $1,500 in monthly sales. Above $1,000/month, the 5% lower base fee on Lemon Squeezy saves creators enough money (typically $50 to $250+ per month) to justify migrating product links and customer management.
How do refunds affect platform fees on Lemon Squeezy vs. Gumroad?
Lemon Squeezy refunds the platform fee when you process a customer refund, meaning you only pay fees on completed sales. Gumroad retains its entire 10% + $0.50 fee even on refunded transactions, resulting in a direct cash loss for sellers on returned orders.
